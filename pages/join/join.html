REQUIREMENTS TO JOIN [QUAD]

1) BE AN ACTIVE PLAYER

You should have been playing NetQuake regularly for a reasonable period of time before asking to join [QUAD].

This is not because we are looking only for highly skilled players. We simply want to know that you really enjoy the game and intend to keep playing.

As stated in the QUAD Code, we do not want ghost members. We want people who can and want to play.

2) JOIN THE QUAD FORUM

You must be registered on the [QUAD] Forum.

The forum is the main place for communication between clan members, announcements, meetings and important clan decisions.

Clan activity is not limited to playing matches. Talk to the other members, take part in discussions and help keep the clan alive.

3) FOLLOW THE QUAD CODE

You must know and respect the QUAD Code.

It is not all that difficult.

HOW TO BECOME A [QUAD] MEMBER

1) CONTACT THE CLANLEADER

Post your request in the appropriate section of the [QUAD] Forum or send a private message to the clanleader.

Tell us who you are, which nickname you use in NetQuake and why you want to join [QUAD].

The clanleader will contact you and the recruitment process will begin.

2) CHOOSE A MENTOR

You will be asked to choose a [QUAD] member as your mentor.

The mentor will play with you, get to know you and follow your progress during the recruitment period.

The mentor is not there simply to judge your skill. The mentor also helps determine whether you fit into the clan and reports your progress to the clanleader.

3) BECOME AN APPRENTICE

You will spend one month as a [QUAD] apprentice.

During this period you will use the special apprentice tag with white letters.

Play with the clan, attend training when possible, talk to the members and show that you want to be part of [QUAD].

Being an apprentice is not only a test of Quake skill. It is a chance for both you and the clan to find out whether [QUAD] is the right place for you.

4) FINAL DECISION

At the end of the apprenticeship, the apprentice, mentor and clanleader will meet and discuss the application.

If everything is clear, a final decision will be made about full membership.

5) IF WE STILL CAN'T DECIDE...

Some traditions deserve to survive.

If there is still doubt after the apprenticeship, the apprentice may face the clanleader in a NetQuake duel.

The apprentice chooses the duel map.

If the apprentice scores at least half as many frags as the clanleader, the apprentice becomes a full [QUAD] member.
