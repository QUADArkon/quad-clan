HOW TO CHALLENGE US

If you want to challenge the [QUAD] Clan, or a single [QUAD] member for an official match, post your request on the [QUAD] Forum and include the following information:

1) WHO ARE YOU?

Tell us your nickname, your clan tag and, if you have one, your clan homepage.

2) WHEN DO YOU WANT TO PLAY?

Tell us your preferred date and time for the match.

Please remember that the date is not confirmed until both sides have agreed on it.

3) WHAT KIND OF MATCH?

Tell us what kind of match you want to play:

  • 1 vs 1
  • team vs team

If it is a team match, tell us how many players you expect on each side.

4) WHERE DO YOU WANT TO PLAY?

Tell us which NetQuake server you would prefer to use.

If you do not have a preferred server, we can agree on one together.

5) WHICH MAPS?

Tell us which maps you would like to play.

Map selection should be agreed by both sides before the match.

6) GAME SETTINGS

Include any particular game settings you want to use.

If nothing different is agreed, [QUAD] prefers its traditional match settings listed below.

7) ANYTHING ELSE?

Tell us anything else we should know before arranging the match.

The important thing is simple: agree on the rules first, then play.

[QUAD] MATCH PREFERENCES

[QUAD] is a NetQuake clan.

Historically, [QUAD] has preferred CRMod for official matches.

Our traditional settings are:

teamplay 2 (friendly fire hurts)
deathmatch 3 (weapon stay)
timelimit 15
no powerups

MATCH TYPES

1 vs 1
One [QUAD] player against one opponent.

Team vs Team
Two teams playing against each other.

No unnecessary game modes. No QuakeWorld. NetQuake only.

ONE LAST THING

We like competitive matches, but a challenge is still a game.

Agree on the server, maps and rules before starting. Play hard, play fair and respect the players on the other side.

Then let Quake decide the rest.
