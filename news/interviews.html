Interview with [QUAD] Arkon

Date: 11/02/2003
Real Name: Filippo
Birthyear: 1971
Location: Torino, Italy
Started Playing: 1996
Finest moments: End of 2001 and beginning of 2002, maybe cause all the finest players have gone :)
Clans Played For: Missing in Action Clan (MiA) for a year, then me and Raistlin started [QUAD] Clan
Controls: Keyboard (WASD) and mouse

Q: Favourite maps/mods and why?
A: Maps: Map e1m2 'cause it's very well balanced and is not that easy to take a zone and hold it for the whole map. After all when I started playing there were no dms in loop in Flashnet server... Mod: CRMod, it's surely the best way to play Quake: GOD save JPG :)
Q: What drove you to play NQ?
A: My girlfriend. I started playing Quake in singleplayer and I didn't love it very much. OK it was fun to hunt monsters but in the end I expected more from the game... One day she told me that in her lunchbreak she and her workmates were playing Quake. I tried to connect (it was really hard the first time) and after that I wasn't able to quit playing.
Q: What did you think of the Euro NQ community at the start?
A: I started with a 33.6K connection so a wasn't able to play outside Italy (and even there lag was great) later I started playing foreign and the average skill of Europeans scared me... It took really a lot of time to be a better player, but it seems that it's not enough...
Q: What did you dislike in NQ?
A: Cheaters: it's too easy to cheat in NQ and a lot of players stopped playing just for this reason.
Q: Do you think the anonimity of the internet is the cause of arguments between players/clans? If not, why not?
A: No, as in real life each played has a personality and is due to that if some fights happened: some people really never understand that Quake is a game and teasing or accusing others other players is for them the best way to have fun. And oh, of course in LANS this never happens, but it needs to be back home to start again.
Q: Did you make real-life friend/enemies in NQ?
A: Friends: a lot, all [QUAD] clanmates and some other Italian players (Death and Xeno of [COBRA] Clan for example). Enemies: a couple. But it's never worth to speak about such people.
Q: Did you consider yourself an addict to the game?
A: Sure, in these years I've collected quite everything has been released for Quake, played quite all Mission packs, spent most of my spare time in playing online and now I'm working on a mod... and damn, all that html for clan homepage... :)
Q: Would you still play if there were many players around?
A: I'm still playing even if there are only a few players left.
Q: Why do you think so many people have left NQ?
A: As I said before cheating first, then can't forget Quake is an old game, most of the players now play Q3A or whatever game has been released later.
Q: What do/did you think of the Euro NQ community at the moment/in the end?
A: "Quake is dying"...seeing most of the servers empty is really sad.
Q: How did you make your clan grow and attract members?
A: Just played, then after a while some italian players asked me to join [QUAD] Clan... It grew by itself and that's what I hoped for in the beginning.
Q: Why did your clan(s) die?
A: [MIA] died for disorganisation: me and some other players wanted it to be better organized, but we found no way to fix this.
Q: How did you handle (possible) conflicts within the clan?
A: Talking. Most of thing can be solved by discussing.

Thanks alot for this interview, Filippo.

Originally published by Oldskool Quake Players.
